# iOS invoice PDF design alignment

Updated November 3, 2026. The reference is the web app at commit `642cfc6`:
`notary-web-app/src/App.jsx` (`downloadInvoicePdf`, `drawPdfStampMark`, and
`PDF_COLORS`) and `src/lib/invoices.js`.

All three iOS export paths—single signing, customer, and custom invoice—now use
`InvoicePDF.swift` through `LedgerPDF.invoice`. The renderer follows the web's
612 × 792 pt Letter page, 44 pt margins, Helvetica/Times typography, navy/brass/
parchment palette, seal, metadata panel, billing columns, five-column table,
alternating row backgrounds, totals, balance panel, payment note, and numbered
footer. Dates, USD amounts, summaries, and filenames follow the web model.

Long invoice numbers and billing names receive extra room to avoid collisions.
Oversized service descriptions continue across pages with repeated table
headings. Native word wrapping and font rasterization can differ slightly from
jsPDF; these are not byte-identical PDFs.

Validation passed:

- 55 existing Swift Testing tests and seven XCTest checks, including the five
  invoice tests and both screen-rendering tests.
- Invoice checks cover all export paths, expected content and balances, an
  80-signing invoice, a description longer than a page, Unicode names, repeated
  column headings/footers, and exclusion of paid/cancelled signings.
- Final focused invoice rerun and top-level Release simulator build passed.
- Tracked changes and new Swift files passed whitespace checks.
